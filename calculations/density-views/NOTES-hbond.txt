System: phenol (donor, O7-H8) + water (acceptor, O14). The lab asks for one partner
with at least 10 atoms, so the shipped General Chemistry example ecceqm-h2o_dimer
(Intermolecular interactions) does not meet the lab's rule; it is the simpler
classroom case and is NOT used for any number here. All numbers below come from
the three ORCA calculations in this directory.

Method: ORCA 6.1.1, RKS B3LYP D3BJ def2-TZVP, Opt+Freq (GeoVib), Hirshfeld + NBO 7 NPA
charges. Set up in the Calculation Editor (theory RDFT, basis def2-TZVP, run type
GeoVib, Theory Details B3LYP + D3BJ, Runtype Details Hirshfeld + NBO, Save) and launched
through ECCE (run_l1c.py hbond). The monomers start from the optimised complex split
in two (lab step 3) -- split by the script, not in the Builder.
Calculations: orca-phenol-water-optfreq (886 s wall), orca-phenol-optfreq (552 s),
orca-water-optfreq (32 s).
